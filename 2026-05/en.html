Turn gratitude into a gift of love

When Jesus was on the cross, He saw His mother and the disciple He loved standing nearby. He said, “Woman, behold your son,” and to the disciple, “Behold your mother.” Even in suffering, Jesus remembered His mother and entrusted love and care to others. Mother's Day reminds us that gratitude can become a visible blessing.
