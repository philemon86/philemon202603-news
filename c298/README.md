# C298 book website

- `index.html`: Traditional Chinese interface and three complete Chinese chapters.
- `zh-cn.html`: Simplified Chinese interface and transcribed complete Chinese chapters.
- `en.html`: English interface; complete Chinese chapters remain in the original Traditional Chinese.
- `style.css` and `reader.js`: shared layout and reading interactions.

The printed book contains Traditional Chinese and English. Website interface language and reading language are independent. All three pages include complete Chinese chapters and published English excerpts as selectable HTML; JavaScript offers chapter selection and Chinese, English, or paired reading. Paired reading is followed by a clearly labeled Chinese continuation; English-only mode hides the continuation. Chinese-only mode reads seamlessly through the complete chapter. Without JavaScript, all three chapters remain readable. Keep text synchronized across language versions, and preserve the existing purchase and newsletter links.
