2026 June Selection

Those held in love,
will also be led by the Holy Spirit

This June selection from Philemon Bookroom highlights three dimensions of faith: being comforted, being built up, and being sent out. This month features one reprinted title and two new books, tracing how faith reaches into the inner life, the family, ministry, and the path of the church.

Well Held responds to the real needs of pastoral care in the church today, reminding us that faith does not only give answers, but also teaches us how to accompany and receive the wounded. To Personally Experience God's Great Love shows how God leads, mends, and completes a life through real human experience. An Exposition of Acts guides readers chapter by chapter through the early church, helping them see how the Holy Spirit builds the church and sends workers so that the gospel moves from Jerusalem to the ends of the earth.
