Course 04 · A Basin at the Table
Course 04 / A Basin at the Table

At this table,
the Lord bent down.

During supper, Jesus rose, poured water into a basin, and began to wash the disciples’ feet. When Peter refused, Jesus said, “If I do not wash you, you have no share with me.” A basin, a towel, and an act of humility open the question of what it means to have a part with the Lord.

C299 returns to John 13, its original language, structure, and context, asking again how footwashing relates to love, humility, salvation, and our relationship with Christ.

Course 05 · After-Dinner Tea
Course 05 / A Cup of Tea After Supper

Bitter at first.
Then the fragrance lingers.

When Elder En-Jung Tseng was ten, he tasted a cup of fine tea brewed by Elder Elisha Huang. The first sip was bitter; a little later, a gentle fragrance and sweetness emerged. He remembered that cup all his life. His essays tell of days both difficult and sweet, and of God's guidance discovered along the way.

“Sometimes strong, sometimes light; sometimes bitter, sometimes fragrant.” — “My First Cup of Fine Tea,” p. 136
