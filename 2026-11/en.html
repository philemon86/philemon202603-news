Course 04 / A Basin at the Table

At this table,
the Lord bent down.

During supper, Jesus rose, poured water into a basin, and began to wash the disciples’ feet. When Peter refused, Jesus said, “If I do not wash you, you have no share with me.” A basin, a towel, and an act of humility open the question of what it means to have a part with the Lord.

C299 returns to John 13, its original language, structure, and context, asking again how footwashing relates to love, humility, salvation, and our relationship with Christ.
